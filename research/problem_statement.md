# Research problem statement

People trying to understand district-court orders may struggle to locate dates, directions, and procedural events across multiple documents. Legal language, long orders, scan quality, and language barriers can increase this difficulty.

## Research question

Can a page-grounded system reliably extract procedural events from a sequence of Karnataka district-court orders and present a traceable chronology in plain English, followed by a carefully evaluated Kannada explanation layer?

## Gap hypothesis to investigate

This project will test whether existing court information services and legal AI tools adequately help ordinary users trace procedural history across multiple orders with explicit page-level evidence, uncertainty labels, and reviewed Kannada explanations. This is a hypothesis for literature and product review, not a claim that no similar system exists.

## Scope

- Karnataka District and Subordinate Courts.
- English and Kannada as initial target languages.
- User-provided PDF orders.
- MVP: text extraction, source-page preservation, then evidence-grounded event extraction and chronology.

## Non-goals

No judgment or bail prediction, legal advice, litigation strategy, automatic deadline calculation, or replacement of advocates.
