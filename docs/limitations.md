# Current limitations

- Selectable-text PDFs only; scanned-document OCR is not implemented.
- Kannada OCR, extraction, and translation are not implemented.
- No procedural-event extraction or multi-order timeline yet.
- No LLM, RAG, vector database, or hallucination checker yet.
- Extracted text can contain reading-order and formatting errors.
- No authentication or production security controls.
- Not suitable for legal advice, deadline calculation, or outcome prediction.

Verify all information against the original court document and an advocate.
