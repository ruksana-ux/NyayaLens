# Privacy and data handling

Court documents may contain sensitive personal information.

## Current prototype

- Uploaded PDF bytes are processed in memory.
- The prototype does not intentionally save uploaded documents to disk or a database.
- Run locally during development.
- Do not upload confidential, sealed, privileged, or sensitive records.
- Never commit real case documents or personal data to this public repository.

## Before deployment

Complete a privacy and security review; define retention and deletion rules; add authentication, authorisation, secure transport, and audit controls; verify legal permissions and reuse terms; redact evaluation data where appropriate; and obtain informed consent before sending documents to external AI providers.

Public availability of an order does not automatically mean unrestricted reuse or redistribution is appropriate.
